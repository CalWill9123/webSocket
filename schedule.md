# Semester Schedule (draft)

Classes: CMPEN 270, CMPSC 122, PHYS 212, MATH 220 — all online.

**Work:** Mon-Thu 7am-1pm, Fri-Sat 10am-5pm, Sun off.

**Free windows:**
- Mon-Thu: ~1:30pm onward (after work + buffer)
- Fri-Sat: mornings before 10am, evenings after 5pm (shorter, split windows)
- Sun: fully open, no work at all

## Weekly template

| Day | Primary focus (evening block) | Secondary (light touch) |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | CMPSC 122 — hands-on coding, not just reading | PHYS 212 review |
| Tue | PHYS 212 — problem sets | MATH 220 review |
| Wed | CMPEN 270 | CMPSC 122 review |
| Thu | MATH 220 — problem sets | CMPEN 270 review |
| Fri | Whichever class has something due soonest (short window, keep it light) | — |
| Sat | Same — deadline-driven, short window | — |
| **Sun** | **Fully open — JS/websocket-chat time, or overflow/catch-up for whichever class is behind** | — |

## Why it's shaped this way

- Each class gets one real focused block a week plus one lighter touch — four classes rotated so nothing goes a full week untouched, without cramming all four into every single day.
- Fri/Sat are deliberately lighter and deadline-driven, not another full rotation — those windows are shorter and coming off/going into a work shift both days, not the time for the hardest focus block.
- **Sunday is JS's home, not a leftover.** Only fully open day, so it's the natural fit for a real websocket-chat session without fighting for time against coursework — and if a class is behind that week, Sunday's also the pressure-release valve instead of overloading a weeknight.
- Not packed edge-to-edge — real room in each evening beyond the study block for food, decompression, actual rest.

## Status

First draft, not fixed law — adjust after a week or two of actually running it against what's fighting real rhythm vs. what's working.
